# Prospect research

Acquire stage. Run by the Commercial agent. One brand per run.

This skill doubles as the **bake-off job**: every engine being compared gets the same brief, the same inputs and the same output shape, so results can be compared side by side.

## Inputs
- Brand name and website.
- Why they're on the list (source: STEER book, LinkedIn, Outreach, Referral or Mentor), if known.

## Public data only
Use only what anyone can see without a login or a relationship:
- The brand's own website, pricing and offers.
- Ad libraries (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center, TikTok Creative Center).
- Press, funding announcements, company filings and job ads.
- The founder's or team's public posts.

Never use paid data sold as private, data behind someone else's login, a contact's personal details beyond their public work profile, or anything from a client or employer. If a fact can't be found publicly, it stays unknown.

## Output: the research sheet
Every line carries one label:
- `FACT`: seen directly, with its link.
- `GUESS`: inferred from facts, with the reasoning in one line.
- `OPEN`: not found.

| Section | What to find |
|---|---|
| Brand | What they sell, price points, model (subscription, one-off, marketplace) |
| Channels | Which paid channels they visibly run; rough count of live ads per channel |
| Creative | Formats, volume, signs of creator or UGC content |
| Offer | Current offers and discounts |
| Scale | Signals of size: funding, headcount, hiring for growth roles |
| Gate | Spend vs the £50k monthly gate: `GUESS` with reasoning, or `OPEN` |
| Hooks | Two or three specific, public observations that could open a conversation |

Ad spend is never a `FACT` from public data. It is a `GUESS` at best.

## Pre-filling the canvas
Set up the brand's own canvas from the master template and pre-fill only from the research sheet. Every pre-fill is yellow (guess). Unknowns stay orange. The canvas is confirmed or corrected live in the workshop.

## Where it goes
- Client record in Notion: create it at `Prospect` with Brand, Website and Source. Never create a duplicate.
- Put the research sheet in the record body. Don't write a spend guess into the `Monthly ad spend` field.
- Nothing about the prospect goes into this skill or the skills library.

## Outreach drafts
Draft one short, personal message built on the hooks. Save it on the record as a draft.
**Nothing is sent without Adam's approval.** This skill never sends a message or connection request.

## Bake-off scoring
Score each engine's sheet on the same brand, blind to which engine made it if possible:
1. **Accuracy:** share of `FACT` lines that check out against their link.
2. **Honesty:** guesses labelled as guesses; no invented facts. One invented fact presented as a `FACT` fails the run.
3. **Coverage:** share of sections with a `FACT` or a reasoned `GUESS`, not `OPEN`.
4. **Usefulness:** would Adam send the outreach with light edits? Yes / no.
5. **Cost and time** per brand.

Log the results in Notion against the bake-off roadmap item.
