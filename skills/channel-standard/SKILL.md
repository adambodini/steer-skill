# Channel standard

The channel standard says how a channel should be run. It is not the numbers (that is the Growth Cockpit) and it is not the accountability forum (that is the SteerCo). It explains why the numbers are where they are.

## The five pillars
Every channel is scored on the same five:
1. **Signal:** what the algorithm is told, and how true it is.
2. **Creative:** volume, testing, graduation, fatigue.
3. **Media buying:** structure, budget lanes, bidding.
4. **Offer:** what is sold, and whether it matches the buying trigger.
5. **Post-click:** from click to conversion, owned by the channel owner.

## Scoring
Each pillar gets one of three levels, with a direction arrow against last month:
- **Basic:** below standard; a known cause of underperformance.
- **Standard:** meets the playbook.
- **Best in class:** ahead of the playbook.

The levels for each channel come from that channel's playbook (e.g. `meta-playbook`). Thresholds start as principles and are set once results back them.

## Monthly rhythm
1. Before the monthly SteerCo, the Delivery agent scores each channel from account access and the playbook.
2. Adam reviews in about 20 minutes and adds judgement.
3. At SteerCo, each owner sees their scores next to their Cockpit numbers and agrees fixes.
4. Scores go into the client's Notion record, so six months of progress is visible.
